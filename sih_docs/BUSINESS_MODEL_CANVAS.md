## 2. Market Sizing & Total Addressable Market (TAM)

### 2.1 The Baseline Problem in India
- **Annual Industrial Fire & Explosion Losses:** ₹3,500–5,200 crore
- **Emergency Services False Alarm Mobilizations:** ₹180–240 crore (68% of all alerts are false alarms)
- **Industrial Environmental Fines & Remediation:** ₹600–900 crore
- **Total Annual Economic Drag:** **₹4,920–6,920 crore**

### 2.2 Addressable Market Breakdown
1. **TAM (Total Addressable Market):** ₹1,540+ crore
   - Pan-India government disaster management + 36 states + 7,000+ major chemical/industrial plants + international South/Southeast Asia corridors.
2. **SAM (Serviceable Addressable Market):** ₹340 crore
   - National Disaster Management Authority (NDMA) + 36 State Disaster Management Authorities (SDMAs) + CPCB + Top 500 heavy petrochemical/steel/power facilities in India.
3. **SOM (Serviceable Obtainable Market - Years 1–3):** ₹45–60 crore
   - Central NTRO/NDMA deployment + 5 high-industrial-density pilot states (Gujarat, Maharashtra, Andhra Pradesh, Odisha, Tamil Nadu) + Top 50 PSU refineries.

## 4. Competitive Moat & Unfair Advantages

1. **Circular Ground-Truth Immunity:** Unlike competitive machine learning models trained on synthetic proximity buffers, PyroSat's 2-stage hierarchy uses Dozier pyrometry physics and 5-gate skeptic verification. It cannot be fooled by agricultural burning next to a chemical plant.
2. **Zero Ingestion Cost:** Proprietary commercial solutions (e.g., Satellogic, Planet, Kayrros) cost $10,000–100,000 per tasking. PyroSat achieves sub-50ms continuous national coverage on 100% free open-access satellites (VIIRS, MODIS, Sentinel-2).
3. **Legally Auditable Evidence Chains:** When CPCB issues an industrial penalty or NDRF orders an emergency evacuation, black-box AI outputs are rejected in courts. PyroSat's polynomial dynamic programming TreeSHAP produces legally admissible, feature-by-feature mathematical proofs.
4. **Immediate Ground Actionability:** Competitors output a raw coordinate dot on a map. PyroSat automatically computes downwind atmospheric dispersion (Briggs/Gaussian), population exposure, chemical hazards (CAMEO/NIOSH), and dispatches a complete Incident Action Plan PDF in under 60 seconds.
